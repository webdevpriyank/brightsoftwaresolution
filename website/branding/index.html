FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a brand identity project?

Typically logo and identity development, colour and typography, visual direction, brand guidelines and key applications such as stationery and social media templates. Scope is tailored to your needs.

Can you refresh our existing brand instead of replacing it?

Yes. Many businesses need a refresh that improves consistency and modernizes the look while keeping recognition.

Do you provide brand guidelines?

Yes. Guidelines help your team and partners apply the brand consistently across every touchpoint.

Can you also build our website and marketing after branding?

Yes. Our software, design and digital marketing teams can take the new brand across your website, campaigns and WhatsApp communication.
